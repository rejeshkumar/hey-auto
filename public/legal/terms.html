4. Fares and Payments

4.1 Fare Calculation

Fares are calculated in accordance with rates set by the Government of Kerala (G.O.(P) No. 14/2022/TRANS):

ComponentRate
Minimum fare (first 1.5 km)₹30
Per km beyond 1.5 km₹15/km
Night surcharge (10 PM – 5 AM IST)+50% of total fare
Onward journey surcharge+50% of amount above minimum
Waiting charge₹10 per 15 minutes (max ₹250/day)

The fare estimate shown before booking is indicative. Final fare is calculated on actual distance travelled.

All ride fares are paid directly by the Rider to the Driver in cash at the end of the ride. The Company does not process or hold any ride fare payments.

Subscriptions are processed via Cashfree Payments. Subscription fees are non-refundable once the access period has started.
