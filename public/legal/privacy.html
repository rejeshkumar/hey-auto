Privacy Policy

Aye Auto — by Navam Works LLP — Effective date: 17 June 2026

Summary: We collect your phone number, live GPS location, and uploaded documents to run the auto-rickshaw booking service. We never sell your data. Drivers keep 100% of fares — we have no financial incentive to misuse your information.

1. Who We Are

Aye Auto is operated by Navam Works LLP, a company registered in India. The app connects riders and auto-rickshaw drivers in Kerala for zero-commission rides. For privacy enquiries contact: privacy@heyauto.in

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Matching you with a nearby auto-rickshaw driver
  • Showing your live location to the rider or driver during an active ride
  • Sending you OTP codes and ride status notifications
  • Verifying driver documents and maintaining road-safety standards
  • Processing and verifying driver subscription payments
  • Resolving disputes, fraud, and safety incidents
  • Improving the app based on anonymised usage patterns

4. Background Location

The Aye Auto driver app collects location data in the background (when the app is minimised) only when the driver has toggled themselves "Online". This allows riders to find nearby available drivers without requiring the driver to keep the screen on. Location collection stops when the driver goes Offline or closes the app.

5. Sharing Your Information

We share your data only in these cases:

  • Matched driver/rider: Your name, vehicle details, and real-time location are shared with the other party during an active ride only.
  • Payment processor (Cashfree): Driver subscription payment references are verified via Cashfree's API. We do not store card or UPI credentials.
  • Cloud infrastructure: Data is stored on Railway (PostgreSQL database) and backed up by Railway's hosting provider. See Railway Privacy Policy.
  • Emergency services: If the SOS feature is used, your location may be shared with Indian emergency services (112).
  • Law enforcement: We comply with valid legal orders from Indian courts or authorities.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Personal identifiers removed within 30 days. Anonymised ride data (fares, distances) retained for business records as required by Indian law.
  • Driver documents: Retained for 12 months after the driver's last active subscription, then deleted.
  • Location history: Live location is not stored. Ride-level start/end coordinates are stored as part of the ride record.

7. Security

  • All API traffic encrypted in transit via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Passwords are never stored — authentication uses OTP only
  • JWTs signed with HS256 and short expiry (15 minutes for access tokens)
  • Driver documents stored with access controls; direct public URLs not exposed
  • OTP brute-force protection: 5 attempts max, then 10-minute lockout

8. Children's Privacy

Aye Auto is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us at privacy@heyauto.in and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@heyauto.in with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification at least 7 days before they take effect. The "Effective date" at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.
