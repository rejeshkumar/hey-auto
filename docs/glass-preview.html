Design Notes
What changes vs current design
Background White → Dark gradient (deep navy/green for map feel). All screens become immersive.
Cards & panels Solid black ink cards → frosted glass (rgba + backdrop-filter blur). Depth and translucency.
Buttons Flat solid → gradient with inner highlight + shadow glow. Feels 3D and pressable.
Tab bar White bg → dark frosted glass. Blends into the map below.
Status pill Flat border → glass pill with blur. Floats over the map naturally.
Trade-off Looks stunning on iOS (native blur support). Android blur is heavier on battery — may need fallback.
