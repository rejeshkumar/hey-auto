How It Works · എങ്ങനെ പ്രവർത്തിക്കുന്നു

  • Rider enters destination in app
  • Nearest driver gets notified
  • Driver accepts or declines — no pressure
  • Rider sees driver on live map
  • Ride completes. Driver keeps full fare.
