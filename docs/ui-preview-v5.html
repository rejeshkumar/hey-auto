The Rules
Gold top bar
Every ink card has a 2px gold gradient line at the top. The signature mark — immediately recognisable across screens.
Key number
The most important number on screen (fare, OTP, earnings) is always #f9b01b and the largest element in the card.
Supporting text
#444 or #666 inside the card — dim enough to not compete, readable enough to scan.
Inactive elements
Everything non-highlighted stays on #F7F7F5 with a 1px #E8E8E8 border. No other colours used.
CTA button
Always #f9b01b background with #0A0A0A text. Gold is only used for the CTA and the ink card accent — nothing else.

Every Black Card in the App
Search bar
Ink background, gold top strip, gold arrow button. The first thing the rider sees and taps.
Fare card
Ink card, gold fare number (₹42 / ₹68). The most important number in the booking flow.
OTP card
Ink card, gold 4-digit OTP. Must be readable at arm's length, shown to driver. Zero ambiguity.
Earnings stats
Ink card, ₹420 in gold. First number a driver sees when they open the app. Should feel like a reward.
Waiting status
Ink card, green top strip (not gold — green = active/alive). Differentiates live state from price info.
Arrived banner
Ink card, green top strip + green text. Green signals progress, not price.
Active subscription
Ink card, gold top strip, days remaining in gold. Feels like a premium membership card.
Selected plan
Ink card, gold top strip, price in gold. Unselected plans stay on white — contrast shows the choice clearly.
Selected payment
Ink card, gold top strip. Unselected payment stays on surface grey. Same pattern everywhere.
USP strip
Ink background, gold text. The brand promise in the most premium treatment.
