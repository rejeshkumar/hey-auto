Design Vision

Two philosophies. One product.

What would each visionary say about Aye Auto's driver experience? Here are the screens they would ship.

Elon Musk

Pure signal. Zero noise.

"A driver is moving at 40 km/h. They need one number in 0.3 seconds. Everything else is distraction. Delete the status bar. Delete the labels. The number IS the label." — Tesla HMI philosophy applied to Aye Auto.

Elon Musk
"If you need to explain a UI element, you've already failed."
  • Pure black background — zero distraction from the numbers
  • One hero number per screen — earnings or fare, nothing competes
  • Tab labels are 1 word maximum — Drive, Earn, Trips, You
  • The ride request shows fare first, route second
  • ACCEPT button says "ACCEPT ₹71" — the action and the reward are the same button
Steve Jobs
"Greet the driver by name. He's not a user. He's a person."
  • Good morning, Rajan — human greeting, not a dashboard
  • Show this week's earnings on the home screen — celebrate progress
  • The rider's name is the headline on the request card — not the fare
  • Real map visible — driver sees their world, not an abstraction
  • "12 trips together" — context that builds trust with repeat riders
