Key observations

#D2B450 — Muted Gold
✅ Feels premium, like a gold badge
✅ Easy on eyes, readable on dark cards
✅ Auto-rickshaw yellow heritage feel
⚠️ Button text: dark ink on gold works well
⚠️ Slightly less visible on white backgrounds
#CC5500 — Burnt Orange
✅ Very high contrast on black cards
✅ Bold and energetic — stands out
✅ Unique — no other auto app uses this
⚠️ Button text: needs white (not dark ink)
⚠️ May look more like a food delivery app
